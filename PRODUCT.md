# Joshua Ogallo’s portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective employers and clients have equal priority, as confirmed by the owner in this conversation. They evaluate Joshua’s software engineering and creative/marketing practice, view work, obtain the relevant résumé and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present two distinct, equally important professional practices with genuine project evidence, clear individual contributions and useful contact routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Astro site provides Software, Creative & marketing, About, CV and Contact pages. Both date-free résumé editions retain native PDF and Word downloads. Contact opens an email draft for the visitor to review and send; it has no delivery backend. Incoming software/project links prefill the enquiry. Direct email and professional profiles remain available.

The owner requested free assets and supplied local videos, logos, mockups and portfolio materials. Paid assets and specialist commissions are not authorised. On 4 November 2026 the owner supplied 48 selected creative images; these are now available as campaign creative, brand content and identity boards with original-file access. Further contribution/context notes may be added progressively.

Collection breadth must be visible on first encounter. Software exposes all nine systems and fourteen websites through inline thumbnail/name indexes before their active presentations, with native project hashes and previous/next controls. Creative pairs its seven featured pieces with a prominent “Browse all 48 artworks” action and three category previews: campaign creative 8, identity boards 5 and brand content 35. The complete collection and each category have native catalog routes under `/creative-marketing/work/`, with original-image access and an enhanced artwork viewer. Local integration checks for this discovery update are recorded in [plan 022](plans/022-collection-discovery.md).

## Brand Commitments

The owner accepted the Refraction homepage, then directed a restrained, minimal, premium treatment for the remaining pages inspired by Jesko Jets. Generic decorative 3D models, busy glass interiors and raised display blocks were rejected. The owner delegated creative implementation and is reviewing the rebuild page by page. The current authorised additions are a professional JO identity/animated favicon, the newly supplied selected creative work and clearer discovery of the complete Creative, Systems and Websites collections.

## Evidence on Hand

Nine supplied software-system mockups, genuine website homepage captures, organisation and product-family logos, Joshua’s portrait, two résumé editions and owner-supplied hero videos are already present. General React/Next.js/Angular and frontend/UI/UX contributions do not establish unverified project stacks, backend authorship or results. Fanta’s Skizia Utamu contribution is creative work and ideation. Agency/employer brand experience does not imply an exclusive direct-client relationship.

## Product Principles

- Keep employers and clients equally well served.
- Use genuine work and confirmed contributions; never invent evidence or outcomes.
- Preserve native navigation, document access and direct contact.
- Respect saved pause and OS reduced motion, with useful static content.
