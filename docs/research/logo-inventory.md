The owner has supplied the marks for inclusion, but the files do not establish that each organization was a direct client. Avoid “Trusted by,” “My clients,” or agency-client mappings inferred from proximity in the folder.

## Presentation and asset suggestions

- Preserve originals and generate web-sized derivatives in the existing local asset pipeline. Loading all originals would add approximately 10.27 MiB before the rest of the page.
- Use contain-based sizing and per-mark optical scale. Equal file dimensions or equal CSS width do not make these lockups appear equally large.
- Give the tall Coca-Cola Beverages Africa lockup a taller or dedicated composition. A short generic logo strip would make its typography tiny.
- Give the Unga Limited composite enough width for its product marks, or present it as a grouped asset. Do not squeeze the entire composite into the same dimensions as a single wordmark.
- Keep the opaque CGA artwork on a compatible light surface. Its colored panels are part of the supplied image; it is not a transparent single-color mark.
- KWS is only 185 × 185. Keep its displayed size modest and include a readable text label.
- Havencrest has dark lettering and strong glow; contrast should be checked in the actual card/background. Hillaxx has substantial surrounding space. The root agent's contact-sheet review should guide crop/padding choices.
- Use the exact capital-L directory paths until derivatives are created. Local Windows resolution can conceal case mismatches that later fail on deployment.
- A concise shared label plus readable organization names is preferable to claiming endorsement through a continuous undifferentiated logo wall.
