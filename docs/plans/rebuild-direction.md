# Rebuild direction — 29 October 2026

The owner has delegated creative direction and authorized a new identity using free assets. The website will have two distinct portfolios: Software Engineering and Digital Creative & Marketing. Brand experience is owner supplied and may have been delivered through employers/agencies; it is not labelled direct client work. Unknown project roles, dates, results, subbrands and technology remain absent. The owner allows campaign placeholders while assembling creative work.

## The idea

On this page, **Joshua Ogallo's initials and two practices become an interlocking sculptural JO**, framed by his name at architectural scale. The visitor can rotate the artwork slightly with a pointer and choose either independent portfolio. The shape opens and reframes with scrolling; it never blocks navigation or project evidence.

Subject inventory: Joshua Odero Ogallo; Nairobi; software engineering; employee-management, commerce, sales and finance products; MCC administration; Uhai; JOOUST Student Apprentice Hub; agency digital-creative practice; Baruchsoft dual engineering/marketing role; Freelancer.com practice; brand-experience breadth; two independent collections.

## Composition and materials

Direction drawn from Taste Skill: **The name as a monument**, seed **253601272**. The name is architectural; the viewer is the quiet frame. Ice-blue ground, ink text, cobalt sculptural material, a small warm vermilion accent for the creative practice. Archivo Variable provides monumental display; Manrope Variable provides readable body/interface text. Both are self-hosted free fonts.

The dealt photographic hero is replaced by authored 3D initials because the user explicitly wants substantial 3D and the meaningful material here is personal identity. The 9–16-tile work wall is replaced by two large independent practice entrances because the user explicitly requires separate portfolios. No stock photography, client logos, or invented campaign artwork will fill missing evidence. An abstract typographic product cover is project artwork, never a screenshot. Campaign placeholders are visibly labelled and have no invented results.

## Page argument / copy deck

- Home: Joshua Ogallo, software engineer and digital creative → original live JO and clear role; choose the relevant portfolio.
- Two practices: software engineering and digital creative/marketing are distinct → two substantial linked visual worlds, not a mixed project feed.
- Software preview: enterprise systems and real websites → selected genuine screenshot and named work; open complete software collection.
- Practice: built through employment, agencies and freelance work → supplied employer roles and biography.
- Contact: talk about a project or role → direct email, CV, professional profile, truthful email-draft form.
- Software: named systems first, websites second → public links only where known; concise project overviews without invented case-study results.
- Creative: digital creative & marketing → service vocabulary supported by supplied roles, complete brand-experience list, clear campaign placeholders for future updates.

## Motion / implementation

Astro static HTML and shared layouts, TypeScript, GSAP for text/image choreography, one lazy Three.js hero scene. Native document navigation with progressive view transitions. No fake loader, autoplay sound, drag-only navigation, cursor replacement, or simulated contact success. Header, direct portfolio links, email and CV are useful before rendering completes.

Pause motion control and operating-system reduced motion. Mobile gets a compact scene and intentional reflow. Frame and asset budgets are validated on the production preview. Unit tests cover email draft construction; browser verification covers navigation, filters, contact feedback, responsive overflow and WebGL fallback. No publishing, paid assets, account creation, or external upload is part of this local rebuild turn.
