## Construction and verification contract

Use one route-local renderer with perspective camera, custom extruded/curved geometry, a shared studio environment, bounded lighting/shadows and demand rendering. Scene builders author objects and camera poses; the director owns scroll, choices, pointer, pause, resize and cleanup. Native scroll remains in control. HTML owns titles, navigation, downloads, forms and evidence. Phone composition is separate, with the object above readable controls. Short viewports use normal flow.

GPU resources are released on page exit, including BFCache suspension, and recreated when a cached page returns. Async generations cannot attach a stale scene. Paused/reduced-motion visitors receive a useful still composition; a missing renderer keeps actual-image or subject-specific vector fallback. No missing original work is replaced with fabricated campaign art.

Review the opening, spatial reveal and resting evidence on desktop and phone for each family. Test choices with pointer and keyboard; drawers and website deep links; Back restoration; CV actions; contact prefill/validation; pause/short-screen paths; missing images and horizontal overflow. Record actual checks and remaining real-device/performance limitations separately. Do not certify optical/material quality from compilation alone.
